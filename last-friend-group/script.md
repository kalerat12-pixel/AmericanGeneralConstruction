# Life as the Last Friend Group — Episode 1

**Format:** Faceless animated comedy short, flat 2D cartoon
**Runtime target:** ~3:45
**Rating:** PG / clean
**Tone:** Fast and funny, one short heartfelt beat near the end

## Logline

Everyone on Earth vanished overnight. The only people left are four teenage best friends.
They now own the entire planet, and they still fight about pizza rolls.

## Cast

| Character | Look | Voice / personality |
|-----------|------|---------------------|
| **MARCUS** | Red hoodie, clipboard, short fade | Self-appointed leader. Makes rules nobody follows. Takes everything way too seriously. |
| **DEV** | Green hoodie, phone always in hand, swoopy hair | Still trying to find WiFi to post updates for followers who no longer exist. |
| **TANK** | Biggest of the four, gray tank top, buzz cut | Quiet. About one line per scene, and it always lands. Usually eating. |
| **JACE** | Blue cap worn backwards, curly hair | Pure chaos. Stole a shopping cart and rode it down the highway. Never stops moving. |
| **RADIO VOICE** | (Heard, never seen) | Mystery voice that sets up Episode 2. |

## SCENE 1 — COLD OPEN (0:00–0:15)

**EXT. EMPTY CITY STREET — DAY**

Silence. Wind. Abandoned cars. Traffic lights blink for no one. A newspaper tumbles past.
Slow push-in down the empty avenue.

From somewhere far away, a voice echoes:

> **JACE** *(distant, shouting)*: WE OWN TARGET NOWWW!

JACE flies into frame riding a shopping cart at top speed, arms up, cap backwards.
He hits a curb and launches into a row of parked carts. **CRASH.** Carts everywhere.

A beat. One wheel spins. A hand rises from the pile, thumbs up.

> **JACE** *(muffled)*: I'm okay!

**SMASH TO TITLE CARD:** *LIFE AS THE LAST FRIEND GROUP*

---

## SCENE 2 — DAY 1: "RULES"

**TITLE CARD:** DAY 1 — RULES

**EXT. SUBURBAN CUL-DE-SAC — DAY**

MARCUS stands on an upturned bucket, clipboard raised. DEV, TANK and JACE stand in a line in front of him.
The lawns are already overgrown. An emergency radio hisses static on the curb.

> **MARCUS**: Okay, listen up. Day one of the apocalypse. We need rules.

> **MARCUS**: Rule one. Nobody goes anywhere alone.

JACE is already climbing into his shopping cart.

> **JACE**: Cool. Bye!

He rolls out of frame. Marcus stares. Continues.

> **MARCUS**: Rule two. We ration the food.

TANK is holding a family-size bag of chips. It is empty. **CRUNCH.**

> **TANK**: It's rationed. Into me.

> **MARCUS**: Rule three. The emergency radio stays on. At all times.

DEV doesn't look up from his phone.

> **DEV**: Bro, it's literally just static.

Dev and Tank wander off. Marcus is now addressing an empty street.

> **MARCUS**: ...Okay. Great meeting, everyone.

**STAMP ON SCREEN:** RULES FOLLOWED: 0

---

## SCENE 3 — DAY 3: THE MALL

**TITLE CARD:** DAY 3 — THE MALL

**INT. EMPTY MALL FOOD COURT — DAY**

The four boys sit at one table piled with every food the food court has to offer.

> **JACE**: Gentlemen. Welcome... to OUR mall.

> **DEV**: Pizza, pretzels, orange chicken, and a smoothie. Best day of my life.

> **MARCUS**: Guys, this is technically stealing.

> **JACE**: Stealing from WHO, Marcus?

Marcus opens his mouth. Closes it. Takes a pretzel.

**INT. MATTRESS KINGDOM — CONTINUOUS**

TANK is asleep on a tower of eleven mattresses. "Zzz."

> **TANK** *(asleep, mumbling)*: Five more years.

**INT. ARCADE — LATER**

Neon lights. MARCUS and JACE face off nose-to-nose in front of the arcade.

> **MARCUS**: I'm calling the arcade. Leader's privilege.

> **JACE**: Nobody voted for you!

> **MARCUS**: Nobody's AROUND to vote!

DEV leans in between them.

> **DEV**: Can we just... share it?

> **MARCUS & JACE** *(in unison)*: NO.

---

## SCENE 4 — DAY 5: DEV'S WIFI QUEST

**TITLE CARD:** DAY 5 — THE SIGNAL

**EXT. WATER TOWER — DAY**

DEV stares up at a very, very tall water tower, phone in hand.

> **DEV**: If there's signal anywhere... it's up there.

The camera tilts up the ladder as Dev climbs.

> **DEV** *(climbing)*: Don't look down. Don't look down. Don't look down.

At the top, wind whipping. Dev holds his phone to the sky. The signal meter flickers... and one bar appears.

> **DEV**: ONE BAR! I HAVE ONE BAR!

> **DEV**: Okay. Posting.

**PHONE SCREEN:** A post: "day 5 of the apocalypse. still thriving. #lastonesstanding"
The like counter sits at **0**. Wind. A single cricket.

> **DEV**: ...Tough crowd.

From far below, a tiny TANK cups his hands.

> **TANK**: Dinner.

---

## SCENE 5 — DAY 9: GROUP CHAT

**TITLE CARD:** DAY 9 — GROUP CHAT

**INT. MANSION LIVING ROOM — NIGHT**

They've moved into the nicest house on the block. All four sit on the same couch, two feet apart, each staring at his phone.

**PHONE SCREEN — GROUP CHAT: "LAST ONES STANDING"** (each message pops in with a ding)

- **MARCUS:** house meeting at 7. mandatory.
- **JACE:** its 6:59
- **MARCUS:** so be there
- **JACE:** bro im on the couch next to u
- **DEV:** can someone pass the chips
- **TANK:** *[sends photo: an empty chip bag]*
- **DEV:** TANK
- **MARCUS:** meeting cancelled

**BACK TO LIVING ROOM.** JACE lowers his phone.

> **JACE**: You guys know we can just... talk. Out loud. Right?

Everyone stares at him. Then, slowly, they all look back down at their phones. **DING.**

- **TANK:** no

---

## SCENE 6 — DAY 14: THE FIGHT

**TITLE CARD:** DAY 14 — THE INCIDENT

**INT. MANSION KITCHEN — DAY**

The freezer door hangs open. MARCUS holds up an empty box of pizza rolls like evidence in court.

> **MARCUS**: Who. Ate. The last. Pizza rolls.

> **JACE**: Don't look at me.

> **DEV**: It was Tank. It's always Tank.

> **TANK**: I was asleep for two days.

> **MARCUS**: Those were the last pizza rolls on EARTH, probably!

> **JACE**: So go to another store! Every store is ours!

> **MARCUS**: It's not about the pizza rolls! It's about TRUST!

> **DEV**: It's a little bit about the pizza rolls.

> **JACE**: That's it. I'm starting my OWN friend group.

> **DEV**: With WHO?

> **JACE**: ...I'll figure it out!

They storm off in four different directions. **DOOR SLAM. DOOR SLAM. DOOR SLAM. DOOR SLAM.**

**ON SCREEN:** SPLIT UP.

---

## SCENE 7 — DAY 15: ALONE (MONTAGE)

**TITLE CARD:** DAY 15 — ALONE

Quiet. Soft, sad music. Each boy alone in the empty world.

**INT. SCHOOL CLASSROOM.** MARCUS stands at a whiteboard covered in rules, addressing rows of empty desks.

> **MARCUS**: Rule forty-one... somebody come back.

**EXT. PARK BENCH.** DEV films himself on his phone. Nobody is watching.

> **DEV**: Hey chat. Day fifteen. ...Chat?

**EXT. HIGHWAY.** JACE's cart rolls slowly down an empty highway. It doesn't even crash.

> **JACE** *(deadpan)*: Wheee.

**INT. GROCERY STORE FREEZER AISLE.** TANK, alone, opens a freezer. Inside: one last box of pizza rolls. He looks at it for a long moment.

*(Tank says nothing.)*

**FOUR-WAY SPLIT SCREEN** of all four of them, alone.

---

## SCENE 8 — SUNSET

**EXT. ROOFTOP — SUNSET**

The rooftop where they used to hang out. Huge orange sun over an empty skyline. MARCUS sits on the ledge alone, radio beside him.
DEV climbs up the fire escape. They see each other.

> **DEV**: Oh. Hey.

> **MARCUS**: Hey.

DEV sits. JACE pops up next.

> **JACE**: Is this a meeting? Nobody told me there was a meeting.

> **MARCUS**: It's not a meeting.

Jace sits. Finally, TANK climbs up holding the box of pizza rolls. He sits down at the end, opens the box, and passes it down the line. Everybody takes one.

A long, quiet beat. The sun sinks.

> **TANK**: Last friend group on Earth... and we still can't agree on pizza.

They all crack up laughing.

> **MARCUS**: Rule one. Nobody goes anywhere alone.

> **JACE**: Okay. That one's actually a good rule.

---

## SCENE 9 — ENDING TAG

**EXT. ROOFTOP — NIGHT**

Stars are out. The boys are sprawled out, half asleep. The emergency radio, which has hissed static all episode, suddenly crackles differently.

> **RADIO VOICE** *(through static)*: ...hello? ...is anyone out there? ...anyone?

All four sit bolt upright. They look at each other.

> **DEV**: Was that... a person?

> **JACE**: DIBS on talking first!

> **MARCUS**: Nobody touches that radio until we make RULES!

**SMASH CUT TO BLACK.**

**ON SCREEN:** EPISODE 2?

**END.**
