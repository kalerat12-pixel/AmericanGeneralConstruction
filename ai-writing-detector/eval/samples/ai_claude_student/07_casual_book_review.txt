So I had to read The Outsiders for English and I was kind of dreading it because the cover looked really old. But it ended up being one of the better books we've read this year, which I did not expect.

The story is about Ponyboy, who is part of a group called the greasers. They're basically poor kids from the east side of town, and they're always fighting with the Socs, who are the rich kids. At first it seems like a pretty simple good guys versus bad guys thing, but it gets more complicated as the book goes on.

My favorite character was probably Johnny. He's really quiet and gets pushed around a lot, but he ends up being super brave when it matters. The part in the church was intense and I actually had to stop reading for a minute after what happened to him. Dally was also interesting because he acts tough all the time but you can tell he really cares about Johnny.

One thing I liked is that the book shows how both sides have problems. Cherry, one of the Socs, tells Ponyboy that things are rough all over, and I think that's kind of the main point. Being rich doesn't automatically make your life easy.

The only thing I didn't love was some of the slang, because it's from the sixties and sometimes it was hard to tell what they meant. But overall I'd give it like an 8 out of 10. It's sad but it's worth reading.
