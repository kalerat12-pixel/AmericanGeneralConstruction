Honestly I think phones should be allowed in school, at least during lunch and between classes. A lot of adults act like phones are the reason nobody pays attention anymore, but I don't really think that's fair. Most kids I know use their phones to text their parents, check the bus schedule, or look up stuff for homework.

Take my school for example. Last year they started a rule where you have to put your phone in a pouch at the start of the day. It sounded okay at first, but then my friend's mom couldn't reach her when her little brother got sick, and she didn't find out until after school. That kind of thing makes the rule feel kind of pointless.

I get that phones can be distracting in class. I've definitely seen people watching videos when they should be taking notes. But I think teachers can handle that the same way they handle any other distraction. If someone is on their phone during a lesson, the teacher can take it for the period. That seems way more reasonable than banning them all day.

Also, learning how to use your phone responsibly is a skill. We're going to have phones at our jobs and in college, so school is kind of the perfect place to practice having self-control with them. Taking them away completely doesn't teach anybody anything.

So yeah, I think schools should let students keep their phones, but with clear rules about when it's okay to use them.
