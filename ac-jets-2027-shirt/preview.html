Ink

The red is matched to PMS 186 C. Black shirts need a white underbase under the red so it stays bright, which counts as a screen at most shops. White and heather shirts print red and black directly.

Files

All type is converted to outlines and every ink sits in its own layer. Inks knock out of each other instead of overlapping. Back art is 12 in wide and the chest hit is 3.5 in wide.

The jet

The jet is an original twin-tail fighter, modelled in 3D and drawn from scratch for this shirt. It is not traced from the school logo.
