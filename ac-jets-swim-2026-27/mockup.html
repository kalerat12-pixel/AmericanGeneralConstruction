AI exploration

Concepts generated with Figma AI to push the Squadron direction. They were used for art direction only: the ideas that held up were redrawn as clean 2-ink vector art above. AI images are not print files.

Production art: vector SVG (all type outlined, inks knocked out) and 300 DPI transparent PNG for every print in every colorway, in squadron/. Ink specs and placement guide are in README.md. The two earlier directions are kept in alternates/.

The jet mark is an original vector drawing based on the AI frames the team picked. Type: Saira, Archivo and Barlow Condensed, all SIL Open Font License.
